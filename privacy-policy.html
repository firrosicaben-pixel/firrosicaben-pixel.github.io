Privacy Policy — Hideaway Uploader

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What this app does

Hideaway Uploader is a personal, single-user tool. It uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API solely to upload the owner's own original videos to the owner's own TikTok account via Direct Post. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we collect

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access token and refresh token) obtained through TikTok's OAuth flow, needed to upload videos on the owner's behalf.
  • Video files the owner chooses to upload.

We collect no data about any other person. We run no analytics and set no tracking cookies.

How data is used

Tokens are used only to authenticate video uploads to the owner's TikTok account. Video files are transmitted to TikTok only when the owner publishes them. Nothing is used for advertising or any other purpose.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, except transmitting the owner's chosen videos to TikTok when publishing.

Data retention

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted if the owner revokes access or deletes the app. Video files are not stored after upload.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through their TikTok account.
