What the app does

Hideaway Uploader is a personal content-publishing tool. It uploads short construction timelapse videos, created by the account owner, directly to the owner's own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own account, with the owner's explicit authorization.
